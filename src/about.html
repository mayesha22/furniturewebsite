A Century of Craft

Our Mission

To honour the art of making — one extraordinary piece at a time.

At Meubles & Co., we believe the spaces we inhabit shape the lives we live. Our mission is simple — to create furniture of such exceptional quality and beauty that it becomes part of a family's story, passed from one generation to the next.

The art of living, made permanent.

Our Story

Born in Kensington.
Built for generations.

Meubles & Co. was founded in 1925 by Henri Beaumont, a French cabinetmaker who arrived in London with little more than his tools and an uncompromising belief in the dignity of handcraft. From a small workshop on Phillimore Gardens, he began producing furniture of exceptional quality for the homes of Kensington and Chelsea.

Nearly a century later, that same workshop ethos endures. Every piece we produce is still drawn by hand, cut by hand, and finished by hand — by a team of artisans who understand that true luxury cannot be manufactured.

"Every joint hand-cut.
Every surface hand-finished.
Every piece built to last not years, but lifetimes."

The Craft

Made by hand.
Finished with intention.

We work with a small network of master ébénistes, upholsterers, and metalworkers — many of whom have spent decades perfecting a single discipline. Our stone comes from quarries in Portugal and Tuscany. Our timbers are sustainably sourced from managed forests across Scandinavia and Central Europe.

Nothing at Meubles & Co. is made at speed. A bespoke commission typically takes between eight and twenty weeks — not because we are slow, but because excellence cannot be hurried.
